💊 How Monobenzone Works

Monobenzone causes free-radical formation in the skin that destroys melanocytes — the pigment-producing cells. It also interferes with tyrosinase activity, the enzyme responsible for melanin production.

The result is gradual, permanent lightening of treated skin to match the depigmented vitiligo patches. Effects are permanent and irreversible.
